Do firms innovate when their rivals consolidate? Supplementary maps

Interactive companions to the data appendix, drawn from the FactSet Revere Supply Chain Relationships records, 2003 to 2021.

  • Firm-level map. Every entity of the rivalry network with headquarters coordinates, one dot each: in blue, a source firm that names at least one competitor; in orange, an entity that is named as a competitor but names none. Hover a dot for the name, the country, the years in which the firm names rivals and the number of firms naming it. The control at the top right toggles the two groups and switches to a clustered view, in which the dots are gathered into counts that split as one zooms in.
  • Country-to-country rivalry flows. Arcs between the countries of the naming and the named firms, with a year slider and a slider for the share of the largest flows shown.
  • Industry-to-industry chord diagram. The rival pairs by the industries of the two firms, with a year slider.
